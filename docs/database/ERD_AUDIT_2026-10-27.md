## Normalization and foreign-key policy

The revision removes independently writable duplicated parents: application candidate/campaign come from its submission; organization comes from the campaign/vacancy; assessment organization comes from vacancy; invitation candidate comes from application; claim extraction comes from its source passage. Criteria, memberships, evidence links, assignments, histories and permissions are separate relations.

This is not a formal proof that every table is strictly 3NF. Frozen application/event snapshots and processing manifests deliberately preserve historical facts. Guarded current-state/count projections (`applications.status`, campaign accepted counts, coverage totals and reservation status) duplicate derivable state for transactional use; canonical event/ledger rows are retained. JSON fields hold versioned payloads, not an invented claim that PostgreSQL has normalized each nested value.

All base tables use bigint identity primary keys except text-keyed sessions. Composite/unique keys prevent repeated versions, mismatched parent tuples, duplicate logical operations and duplicate campaign/account submissions. Foreign keys prevent dangling referenced parents. Cascade is used for owned subordinate records; restrictive/default NO ACTION links protect cross-purpose evidence and history. Some deletion triggers further restrict cascades until their retention purpose permits removal.

Minimal purge tombstones deliberately do not reference the deleted scope. Audit entity IDs are polymorphic retained metadata rather than relational FKs. These are intentional retention boundaries, not an assurance that all future log payloads are safe. `application_decisions` is a view of canonical events, not another physical table.
